# Max Gupta

**Enterprise Data & AI Leader**  
Data Platforms | Architecture & Engineering | Governance & Transformation

Dallas-Fort Worth, Texas

I help organizations modernize data platforms, strengthen trusted analytics, and connect strategy with accountable delivery. My background spans approximately 20 years in technology, including more than a decade in banking leadership at Citi and JPMorgan Chase.

My work brings together enterprise data architecture, engineering delivery, data products, quality, lineage, and controls in regulated environments. My independent work explores how organizations can adopt, organize, orchestrate, govern, and scale a mixed workforce of humans and AI agents.

- **Engineering and delivery leadership:** translating business priorities into roadmaps, delivery decisions, and sustainable ownership.
- **Trusted data and governance:** data quality, lineage, evidence, and controls for reliable decisions.
- **Enterprise AI:** operating models for bounded agent authority, independent checking, human oversight, and outcome acceptance.

## Selected independent work

### TRANTIATE

Creator of TRANTIATE, an operating methodology for work performed by humans and AI agents. It connects objectives, responsibilities, authority, evidence, independent assurance, and acceptance through four roles: Seer, Maker, Checker, and Buyer.

[Explore the methodology and publications](https://www.trantiate.com/) · [Research](https://www.trantiate.com/research) · [Resources](https://www.trantiate.com/resources)

### Governed AI-agent workflows

Exploring vendor-neutral Maker-Checker workflows for website improvement, with work orders, review records, evidence, and human decisions. The emphasis is on making delegated work traceable and accountable.

### Trantiator

Developing the product direction for a vendor-neutral software layer to operationalize TRANTIATE. This is ongoing work; descriptions of intended capabilities represent product direction rather than a claim of a production-ready platform.

### Hello Humans / HelloHums

An initiative exploring professional adaptation to AI, workforce readiness, and practical opportunities for human-AI collaboration.
